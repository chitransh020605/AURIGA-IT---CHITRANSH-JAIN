What it is

DabbaLedger is a subscription and billing ledger built for one specific job: a tiffin owner who delivers lunch on weekdays, needs to pause a customer without a fuss, and needs a correct bill at month-end without doing the arithmetic by hand.

Subscriptions by phone number

Every customer is found and managed by their phone number, the way an owner already thinks about their list.

Pause and resume, mid-month

Log a travel week or a festival gap in one step. The days simply stop counting toward the bill.

Pro-rated billing

The plan price is split across the weekdays in the month, so a customer only ever pays for lunches actually sent.

Active vs. paused, at a glance

The dashboard lists who's currently being served and who's sitting out, sortable and searchable.

Month-end report

One screen generates every customer's bill for the month, with the delivered-day count shown alongside.

Search, sort, paginate

Built for a list that will outgrow a notebook — find a customer or a bill in seconds, not scrolling.

Who it's for, and how it helps

Built for the person running the tiffin service day to day, not a chain — one owner, a phone full of customer numbers, and a kitchen that doesn't stop for admin.
